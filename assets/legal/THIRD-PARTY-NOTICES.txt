gpui 0.2.2
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GPUI
Copyright 2022 - 2025 Zed Industries, Inc.
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE-APACHE).

This directory is a modified copy of the gpui crate from Zed v1.28.1
(https://github.com/zed-industries/zed, crates/gpui, commit e8bc16ace).
zyuapp changed it in 2026. The changes:

- The manifest stands alone: Zed's workspace settings are written in.
- Page zoom: layout pixels map to zoom-scaled window points; border widths
  snap to whole pixels at the zoom.
- Text: letter spacing; Chromium's line heights, baselines, line breaks and
  text indent; truncation keeps shaped lines; text layouts keep the
  inherited colour and decorations so hover, press and focus styles recolour
  them at paint; dotted and dashed underlines with a set offset.
- Layout: lengths and centred offsets in a 1/64px layout unit; boxes snap to
  whole pixels from the root's position; unrounded layout origins; scroll
  overflow measured before rounding.
- Lists: estimated heights for unmeasured rows, item remeasurement, rows
  stacked at whole pixels and browser-style scroll anchoring.
- Live views that do not dirty their ancestors; cached views replay their
  last frame; the view cache key includes fade mask, opacity and transform.
- Style transitions and enter animations.
- Paint-time transforms (scale, translate, rotate) applied to primitives,
  masks, hitboxes and accessibility bounds.
- Backdrop filters, edge fade masks, radial gradients, layered shadows and
  hairline SVG strokes.
- Accessibility: element roles and names added to the window's AccessKit
  tree (src/accessibility.rs, src/accessibility/).
- Browser-style keyboard focus: Tab stops, focus-visible rings, tab order
  groups, inert subtrees, focus traps, Enter/Space activation.
